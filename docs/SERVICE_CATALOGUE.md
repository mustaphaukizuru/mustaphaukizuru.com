## 0 · How this catalogue is built

Every micro-service below is a **SKU**: fixed scope, fixed price, fixed delivery window, defined exclusions. Nothing is sold hourly. This is deliberate — the operator is capacity-constrained (~55 billable hours/month alongside a full-time role), so the portfolio is engineered for **fixed-scope delivery, recurring retainers, and zero-marginal-cost products** rather than open-ended consulting.

**Revenue ladder — every SKU occupies one rung:**

```
TRIPWIRE            CORE PROJECT           RETAINER              PRODUCT
MXN 8–15k     →     MXN 25–90k       →     MXN 6–25k/mo    →     MXN 900–3,500
acquire             fund                   stabilise             scale
```

**Pricing convention:** all figures in MXN, excluding IVA. Prices are internal anchors — never published on the website. Publish outcomes; price in the call.

**Effort:** estimated delivery hours for a single operator. Used to protect margin and prevent capacity overrun.

**Legend — revenue model:**
`ONE` one-off · `RET` recurring retainer · `HYB` setup fee + retainer · `PRD` digital product · `ADV` advisory (existing clients only)

## 02 · Data Compliance & IT Governance
*Cumplimiento de Datos y Gobernanza de TI*

**Positioning:** the tripwire. Not a profit centre — a **customer acquisition channel** that produces a paid audience and a documented list of their problems.

**Timing advantage:** a new LFPDPPP was published 20 March 2025 and took effect 21 March 2025, repealing the 2010 law. INAI was dissolved; oversight moved to the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno**. The definition of *responsable* expanded to include processors, and *aviso de privacidad* requirements changed. Effectively every PyME in Mexico is now out of compliance and unaware of it. This window will not stay open.

> ⚠️ All marketing, copy and deliverables must reference the **2025 LFPDPPP**. Any link to the repealed 2010 statute is a credibility failure in front of a qualified buyer.

**Buyer:** director general, legal/administrative lead, any company holding customer data.

## 04 · Cloud, Infrastructure & Reliability
*Nube, Infraestructura y Continuidad*

**Positioning: rebuilt, deliberately downscoped.** The original framing (enterprise on-prem → AWS/Azure/GCP migration, zero-trust hardening) targets a buyer who requires partner certifications, professional liability insurance, client references and a 24/7 SLA. A solo consultant loses those bids and burns months learning it.

The *underlying need* is real — but the winnable buyer is the **PyME**, and the PyME does not buy "zero-trust architecture." They buy *"que no se me pierda la información y que nadie entre a mis sistemas."* Same skills. Different buyer, different language, winnable deal.

## 08 · Launch Gates — no revenue is possible until these close

| Gate | Why it is blocking |
|---|---|
| **RFC + CFDI 4.0 invoicing** | Without a factura, no formal Mexican company can deduct the expense. This alone disqualifies you from most mid-market deals. |
| **Immigration status for independent work** | Residency tied to a school employer may not permit independent consulting income. Verify with an abogado migratorio. |
| **Employer conflict of interest** | Category 05 sells into the employer's own sector. Written clarity required first. |
| **Spanish-language sales capability** | The PyME buyer negotiates in Spanish. Either invest in it, or deliberately target the bilingual segment (US companies with Mexican operations, international schools, expat founders) where English is an asset rather than a gap. |

## 09 · 90-Day Sequence

1. **Weeks 1–2** — Close the launch gates (§08). Nothing else matters until these are done.
2. **Weeks 3–4** — Ship **02** (C1 diagnostic). Fastest to market, no delivery risk, builds the lead list.
3. **Weeks 5–8** — Ship **01** with **one SKU only: A1**. Sell three. Resist the urge to offer the full menu.
4. **Weeks 9–12** — Package **06** (D1, D2) from the artifacts produced in weeks 3–8. **This is the compounding step. Do not skip it.**
5. **Category 05** launches on the school budget cycle, once the conflict-of-interest gate is cleared.

**Categories 03 and 04 are not marketed in the first 90 days.** They are quoted reactively when work in 01, 02 or 05 surfaces a build or an infrastructure need.
